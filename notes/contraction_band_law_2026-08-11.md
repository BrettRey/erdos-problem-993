# Contraction forms on trees: two conjectures killed, what survives

Date: 2026-08-11 (late). Companion script:
`verify_contraction_band_law_20260811.py`; results:
`results/contraction_band_law_20260811.json`. Produced with substantive
generative-AI assistance (Fable session); exact integer arithmetic
throughout. **Two successively weaker universal conjectures were stated
and killed the same night**; this note records both kills, the data, and
the statements that remain standing.

## The forms

For a tree `T` and an edge `e`, write `b = coeffs I(T)`,
`c = coeffs I(T/e)`, `N = deg I(T)`, and

```
U_k = c_k b_k - c_{k-1} b_{k+1}
V_k = c_k b_k - c_{k+1} b_{k-1}
W_k = 2 c_{k-1} b_k - c_k b_{k-1} - c_{k-2} b_{k+1}
```

**Killed conjecture 1 (clean laws, all trees).** `V_k >= 0` and
`W_k >= 0` at every index, every tree, every edge. Died on non-LC
composites (below).

**Killed conjecture 2 (absolute band law).** All failures of all three
forms confined to reflected depth `N - k <= 2`. Died within the hour on
multi-break composites: attaching a pendant leaf to pattern-family trees
with 1, 2, and 3 consecutive LC breaks (orders 177 to 315) produces
`U`-failures to reflected depth 23, `V` to depth 5, `W` to depth 4
(6,432 contraction pairs; depth histograms in the addendum below). The
failure depth scales with the component's internal synchronization-band
depth, which `notes/absorption_margin_probe_2026-08-11.md` showed grows
linearly in the family parameter. There is no absolute constant strip.

## Why these forms

Rooting at the contracted edge `e = (u, v)` gives, with
`A_w = I(T_w - w)` and `D_w = I(T_w - N[w])` for the two components
`T_u, T_v` of `T - e`,

```
I(T)   = A_uA_v + x(D_uA_v + A_uD_v)
I(T/e) = A_uA_v + xD_uD_v
```

which is exactly the `B`, `C` shape of the C2 base facts
(`notes/c2_bounded_pendant_core_2026-08-08.md`); the two-hub case is the
special case where all pieces are products of path polynomials. The
assembly lemma of `notes/c2_single_index_laws_2026-08-11.md` applies
verbatim: `I(T/e)` log-concave plus `V` and `U` at the relevant indices
imply the partial-synchronization inequality for `(I(T/e), I(T))` at
every pair, and `W` covers the diagonal for `(xI(T/e), I(T))`. Through
the subdivision identity `I(T_e) = I(T) + xI(T/e)`, a universal bound
on where these forms can fail would make the assembly lemma a
subdivision-stable transfer device. The kills below show the failure
region is NOT an absolute O(1) top window on general trees; it scales
with the pathology of the pieces. The geometry still matches the
pattern-family synchronization band
(`notes/partial_sync_obstruction_2026-08-11.md`): failures live in a
head region, but its depth grows with the component parameter.

## Evidence

All arithmetic exact. Four sweeps, the fourth being the one that killed
conjecture 2 (all regenerated by the companion
script):

1. **Exhaustive.** All 32,506 non-isomorphic trees on 3 to 16 vertices
   (geng), all 464,871 (tree, edge) contraction pairs. `V` never fails.
   `W` fails 12 times, `U` in a minority of cases; every failure of
   either sits at reflected depth <= 2.
2. **Random.** 400 random trees to 18 vertices, all edges: same
   picture.
3. **Adversarial non-LC composites.** Kadrawi-Levit trees `KL(3,k,k)`
   for `k = 4, 5, 6`, whose independence polynomials already break
   log-concavity, with a pendant path of length 1 to 3 attached at
   every vertex; all edges of every composite (8,466 pairs). Here `V`
   fails too, 80 times, every failure at reflected depth exactly 1; `W`
   fails at depths 0 and 1; `U` at depths 1 and 2.

Across roughly 478,000 contraction pairs, no violation of any form at
depth 3 or deeper.

## Kill history and addendum: the multi-break stress

Kill 1: conjecture 1 survived the exhaustive `n <= 16` sweep for `V` but
died the same hour on KL non-LC composites: once a component is
non-log-concave, `V` picks up depth-1 failures and `W` depth-0/1.

Kill 2: the retreat to an absolute depth-2 band survived exhaustive,
random, and KL-composite testing (~478k pairs, deepest failure depth 2)
and then died on the very next stress named in this note's first draft:
multi-break pattern components. Bases `(T_{1,2}:S_{2,4})_2^{(9)}` (one
break), `(T_{1,3}:S_{2,4})_2^{(16)}` (two breaks),
`(T_{1,7}:S_{2,5})_2^{(13)}` (three breaks), one pendant leaf at eight
spread vertices each, all edges contracted (6,432 pairs). Pooled
violation depth histograms:

```
V: {1: 205, 2: 302, 3: 949, 4: 427, 5: 32}
W: {0: 108, 1: 3102, 2: 1770, 3: 883, 4: 649}
U: depths 1..23, mass concentrated at 1..5, tail 5..23
   (e.g. {5: 309, 12..17: ~12-18 each, 18..23: 2 each)
```

All observed failures remain far inside the top third of the sequence
(deepest 23 against degrees 90 to 160+), matching the geometry of the
component families' own synchronization bands, whose depth grows
linearly in the family parameter (absorption probe, same day). So the
correct universal statement, if one exists, must normalize depth
relative to the component pathology or the Levit-Mandrescu zone, not by
an absolute constant. That statement is deliberately NOT conjectured
here; the next session should measure how contraction-form failure
depth scales with the component parameter before freezing anything.

## What survives

1. **The C2 clean laws** (`notes/c2_single_index_laws_2026-08-11.md`):
   for arm pairs with all pieces products of path polynomials, `V` and
   `W` hold everywhere and `U` off reflected depth <= 3. Unrefuted by
   everything above (the kills all require non-log-concave pieces,
   which the C2 class cannot produce). The assembly lemma and the C2
   program are untouched by the kills.
2. **The A/D structural identity**: rooting at the contracted edge
   always gives the two-hub `B`/`C` shape, so the assembly lemma
   applies to any tree edge whenever its single-index hypotheses hold.
   What died is only the claim that those hypotheses hold universally
   with an absolute head strip.
3. **Exhaustive small-tree fact**: for every tree to 16 vertices and
   every edge, `V` never fails and all `U`, `W` failures sit at depth
   <= 2 (464,871 pairs). A bounded-order theorem to this effect may be
   provable and useful for small-case handling.

## Limitations

Finite corpus throughout; the multi-break stress used one gadget (a
single pendant leaf) at eight attachment points per base. The
depth-scaling reading (failure depth tracks component band depth) is a
visual match across three bases, not a fitted law. Nothing here proves
or refutes the C2 facts or bears directly on Erdos Problem 993's truth.
