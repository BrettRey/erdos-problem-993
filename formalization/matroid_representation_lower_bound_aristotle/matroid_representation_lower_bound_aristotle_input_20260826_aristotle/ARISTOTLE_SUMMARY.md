What was proved:

- **G0 (required)** — `MatroidRep.le_card_of_represents`: for an arbitrary type `α`, arbitrary `G : SimpleGraph α` and arbitrary `M : Fin k → Matroid α` with `∀ S, G.IsIndepSet S ↔ ∀ i, (M i).Indep S`, every vertex `v` whose neighbourhood is independent satisfies `deg v ≤ k`. No hypothesis on the ground sets `(M i).E` is needed — the representation hypothesis applied to singletons already puts every vertex in every ground set, so that assumption was dropped rather than assumed. A `Fintype` restatement `G.degree v ≤ k` is also given (`degree_le_of_represents`).
  The proof follows the intended route: singletons are independent so no matroid has a loop (`indep_singleton_of_represents`, `isNonloop_of_represents`); a dependent pair is then a circuit (`isCircuit_pair_of_represents`); circuit elimination on `{v,u}` and `{v,w}` yields the dependent pair `{u,w}`, contradicting independence of the neighbourhood (`not_dep_pair_of_dep_pair`); the resulting injection from the neighbourhood into `Fin k` gives the count.
- **G1 (reached)** — `le_card_of_represents_cliqueFree` for triangle-free graphs, via `isIndepSet_neighborSet_of_cliqueFree`, plus the tree corollary `le_card_of_represents_isTree`. The tree-to-triangle-free step was cheap: `cliqueFree_three_of_isAcyclic` builds the 3-cycle from a triangle directly, so both forms are delivered.
- **G2 (reached, reduction confirmed)** — modelling ground-set enlargement by an embedding `e : α ↪ β` with `∀ S : Set α, G.IsIndepSet S ↔ ∀ i, (N i).Indep (e '' S)`, comparison along `e` (`represents_comap`) produces a representing family of matroids on `α` with the same `k`, so G0 applies unchanged (`le_card_of_represents_of_embedding`). Because a minor of a matroid is again a matroid on the same type, this also covers contraction: `le_card_of_represents_minor` states the bound for representations by arbitrary minors `((P i).contract (C i)).restrict (R i)`. So the conjectured reduction is confirmed, not refuted.

The upper bound was, as requested, left out of scope.
