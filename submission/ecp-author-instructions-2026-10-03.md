## What they settle for this submission

- **System:** EJMS, <https://www.e-publications.org/ims/submission/>. A first-time
  user registers once. Someone already registered in another role or journal
  adds the "ECP author" role to the existing profile.
- **Format:** LaTeX with the ECP template, submitted as a **PDF**. The page does
  not ask for source files at submission, so the source bundle is held for
  acceptance.
- **Length:** short papers, "those less than 12 pages", should go to ECP first.
  The venue record cites the 2025 IMS editorial report: "within 12 pages, at
  most 13".
- **AI use:** neither page has an AI-specific rule. The manuscript's page-1
  footnote and acknowledgement disclose AI use.
- **Licence:** published articles are CC BY. The supplement on Zenodo is
  separately MIT-licensed.
- **Fees:** none. Voluntary donations to the Open Access Fund are accepted.
- **Eligibility:** current EJP/ECP board members may not submit. Brett is not on
  the board.

## Journal page (verbatim)

> Electronic Communications in Probability
> The Electronic Communications in Probability (ECP) publishes short peer-reviewed research articles in probability theory. Its sister journal, the Electronic Journal of Probability (EJP), publishes full-length articles in probability theory. Short papers, those less than 12 pages, should be submitted to ECP first. Please see the Manuscript Submission page for new instructions. EJP and ECP share the same editorial board, but with different Editors in Chief.
>
> This journal is a free access co-sponsored journal of the Institute of Mathematical Statistics (IMS) and the Bernoulli Society.
>
> Author or publication fees are not required.  Voluntary fees or donations to the Open Access Fund are accepted. Expenses not covered by voluntary payments are paid for by the co-sponsoring societies as a service to the community.
>
> All works in this journal are licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License.
>
> Current members of the EJP/ECP board are kindly requested not to submit manuscripts (including co-authored ones).

## Manuscript Submission page (verbatim)

>
> Manuscripts must be written in Latex using the ECP template. Please see the LaTeX support page for IMS publications using the template. Manuscripts must be submitted in pdf form.
